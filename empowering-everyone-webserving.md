# Empowering Everyone — Local Commerce, Bottom Up

**WebServing.com** | **EmpoweringEveryone.com** | **WebClerk.com**

## The Problem

A customer searches for a product online. Amazon shows it from a warehouse 800 miles away. The hardware store 3 miles away has 12 on the shelf — but that inventory is **invisible to the web**.

- **Resources drain from the local economy.** The sale, the tax revenue, the job — gone.
- **Customer relationships are surrendered to Amazon without compensation.**

| Centralized Marketplace | Desktop Hosting |
|------------------------|-----------------|
| 15–30% marketplace fees | Zero marketplace fees |
| Your data belongs to them | Your data stays on your machine |
| Your customers belong to them | Your customers are yours |

## The Solution

Every business runs its own commerce system on its own hardware. **Sovereign.** WebServing.com routes customer searches to every local store within 1–10 miles. No central catalog. No fees. The router, not the owner.

**4 steps:** (1) Install WebClerk — free, open source. (2) Get a domain (~$10/year). (3) Buy a Mac Mini ($1,500–$1,900). (4) Load your inventory — AI assists, free HQ support for 2 months.

| | Cost | vs. Amazon/Shopify |
|-|------|--------------------|
| Marketplace fees | **$0** | Amazon: 15–30% per transaction |
| AI assistance | **$14/mo** (5 staff) | Shopify: $79–399/month |
| Hardware | **~$1,700** one-time | Both own your customer relationship |

> *The individual is sovereign. Institutions are agents with limited, enumerated, revocable permissions.* — Divided Sovereignty
