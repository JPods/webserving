How it works

  1. Install WebClerk (free, open source) or another Desktop Hosting application.
  2. Get a domain if you don't have one. We recommend Cloudflare or Hostinger.
  3. Buy a Mac Mini or IT15 with 24+ GB of RAM ($1,500 - $1,900).
  4. Load your data:
    • Link your current inventory system to WebClerk
    • Or export your data and ask WebClerk to upload it
    • WebClerk has integrated AI to assist
    • WebClerk HQ offers parsing support — no cost for 2 months
  5. You are now Web Serving your own data from your own server. No middleman. No giving your customer relationships to Amazon.
